KABASHI IPTV V14.13 — Stream Compatibility

Only playback compatibility was changed.
- Media3 now tries: original direct source, its alternate extension, Xtream TS, and Xtream HLS.
- VLC Compatibility mode receives all candidate URLs and retries them automatically.
- VLC sends IPTV-friendly User-Agent and Referrer options.
- The successful URL is remembered per channel for faster playback next time.
- Existing design, cards, categories, EPG, favorites, and navigation remain unchanged.
