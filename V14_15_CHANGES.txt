KABASHI IPTV V14.15 - VLC BUFFERING / LAG FIX

- Loading spinner is delayed briefly and disappears on Playing or near-complete Buffering.
- Separate UI and playback timers prevent the controls timer from cancelling stream fallback checks.
- VLC cache increased to 2200 ms for unstable live channels.
- Late frames may now be dropped/skipped so playback can catch up instead of falling behind.
- Adds HTTP reconnect/continuous options.
- If a stream does not start, VLC retries once and then moves to the next candidate URL.
- Old stream is stopped and pending timers are cancelled before switching channels.
- VLC channel switching and external-player setting remain included.
