KABASHI IPTV V14.12 — VLC AUDIO EXPERIMENT

Only audio fallback was changed.
- Media3 remains the default fast player.
- New VLC AUDIO button opens the same stream in an internal LibVLC player.
- Intended for channels whose audio codec is not decoded by the Fire TV MediaCodec stack.
- APK size will be significantly larger because native VLC codec libraries are included.
- All design, Kosovo cards, login, favorites, EPG, and navigation remain unchanged.
